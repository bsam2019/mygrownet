## 1. Executive Summary

GrowStream is a digital entertainment and creator monetization platform built by MyGrowNet. The platform provides a dedicated marketplace for Zambian entertainment creators to distribute and monetize premium content, while giving audiences affordable access to locally produced entertainment.

The problem GrowStream addresses is not a lack of places for creators to publish. Platforms such as YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, and Instagram already provide global distribution. The real gap is that many Zambian creators build large, engaged audiences without a reliable way to earn income from that work.

GrowStream creates the missing layer between audience building and income generation — creators use free social platforms for discovery and growth, and GrowStream for premium content, monetization, and professional distribution.

The platform will launch focused on Zambia, then expand into Southern Africa and other African markets once the model is proven.

## 2. Vision

To become Africa's leading digital entertainment marketplace — enabling creators to earn from their content while connecting audiences with authentic local entertainment.

## 3. Mission

To provide African creators with the tools, audience, and economic opportunities required to turn entertainment creation into a sustainable business.

## 4. Market Opportunity

### The Creator Challenge in Zambia

Zambia has a growing entertainment industry — comedians, skit creators, filmmakers, musicians, drama groups, and digital content creators — but many face the same structural challenges:

- **Limited monetization** — Creators may have thousands of followers, high engagement, and viral videos, yet still earn little or no direct income from their work.
- **Limited professional distribution** — Most creators distribute through Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and WhatsApp — platforms strong on visibility but weak on local monetization pathways.
- **Limited investment** — Many creators lack funding for better equipment, production teams, editing, and marketing — capping the quality and consistency of what they can produce.

GrowStream is built to close these three gaps directly.

5. **Music & Music Videos** — Official music videos, live performances, acoustic sessions, artist interviews, behind-the-scenes content. Managed carefully due to licensing requirements — only creators with verified rights may publish.

## 9. Content Acquisition Strategy

GrowStream will not launch as an open-upload platform. The launch approach is curated, prioritizing quality and rights certainty over volume.

Uploading is free for creators — there is no fee, commission, or hosting charge to publish on GrowStream. MyGrowNet is responsible for vetting every submission before publication, ensuring the platform maintains a consistent standard for production quality, content appropriateness, and rights ownership.

## 14. Monetization Model

### Core Model: Watch-Time Revenue Share

Creators do not pay to publish on GrowStream. Instead, creator earnings are funded directly from premium subscription revenue and distributed according to minutes watched by premium users — the same royalty-pool mechanism used across music streaming, where the platform retains a share and distributes the remainder to rights holders in proportion to their share of total consumption.

This ties creator income directly to real, verified audience engagement rather than follower counts, registrations, or flat fees — addressing the core problem GrowStream exists to solve: creators with large audiences but no reliable way to earn from them.

### Revenue Split: 70% to Creators

GrowStream allocates **70% of premium subscription revenue** to the creator pool, with the remaining **30%** covering platform operations, payment processing, and community growth rewards.

This rate is benchmarked directly against current market standards:

| Benchmark | Creator Share |
|---|---|
| Music streaming royalty pools | ~70% to rights holders (platform retains ~30%) — the closest structural precedent |
| Twitch (top partner tier) | 70/30 in creators' favor (new/non-partner creators default to 50/50) |
| YouTube Partner Program | ~55% of ad revenue |
| Kick | 95% creator share — aggressive VC-funded tactic, not a sustainable baseline |

A 70% creator share positions GrowStream at the top tier of what established platforms offer — matching the music-streaming royalty standard and Twitch's premium partner rate — making it a genuine, defensible differentiator rather than an arbitrary number.

### Launch Tiering Decision

Several platforms treat a top-tier split like 70% as earned rather than given by default — Twitch reserves it for creators who clear an engagement threshold, with new creators starting lower. GrowStream's curated launch approach changes that calculus: because the initial ~40 partners are hand-selected and vetted before onboarding, offering all of them 70% from day one is a reasonable and justifiable launch incentive rather than an unearned rate.

What should be decided deliberately now, rather than left to default: whether 70% remains the flat rate for every creator as the platform opens more broadly, or whether it becomes a top tier that new, unvetted creators work up to once GrowStream moves beyond curated partnerships. This matters because walking a rate down from 70% later is a far harder conversation with creators than starting lower and growing it — the launch cohort's expectations will anchor to whatever rate they are given first.

### Additional Revenue Streams

The watch-time pool is the default earning mechanism every creator receives automatically. The following are optional, additive streams creators can layer on top:

- **Pay-per-view** — for premium releases such as a new movie, a comedy special, or a concert
- **Creator subscriptions** — fans support specific creators directly (e.g. "Subscribe to Comedian X for exclusive content")
- **Tips & support** — fans send direct one-off support to creators
- **Sponsorship marketplace** — brands sponsor shows, creators, or events

### Open Questions Before Launch

- Exact payout formula: pool revenue ÷ total premium minutes watched platform-wide, multiplied by each creator's own minutes watched
- Whether a minimum guaranteed payout is needed for the launch cohort while the premium subscriber base — and therefore the pool — is still small
- Anti-gaming safeguards to define what counts as a valid watched minute (e.g. minimum session engagement, unique-viewer weighting) before the model is exploited
- Final subscription price point, validated against real willingness to pay rather than set by assumption

## 15. MyGrowNet Community Growth Model

The referral system is not GrowStream's public identity — GrowStream remains, first and foremost, an entertainment platform. The referral and community model operates in the background as a growth mechanism, not a marketing message.

**User journey**
- Viewer joins and uses GrowStream
- Viewer enjoys content
- Viewer discovers the referral opportunity
- Viewer chooses whether to promote the platform

**Reward principles**

Rewards are tied to genuine platform activity, not to registrations:

- Active users
- Verified content consumption
- Paid subscriptions
- Creator engagement

The system is designed to reward platform growth, not simple recruitment.

## 16. Technology Architecture

GrowStream uses **Cloudflare Stream** as its video infrastructure, built as a shared capability within the MyGrowNet Platform Core rather than a GrowStream-specific system — consistent with how identity, payments, and notifications are centralized across MyGrowNet.

| System | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Video Service** | Video storage, encoding, streaming, delivery, and analytics |
| **Content System** | Movies, creators, categories, rights management, moderation |
| **Commerce System** | Subscriptions, rentals, payments, creator payouts |
| **Community Growth System** | Referrals, rewards, ambassador programs |

These four systems interact but remain architecturally separate, so GrowStream can evolve each independently and other MyGrowNet applications can reuse the underlying services in future.

## 17. Content Moderation & Rights Management

Entertainment content carries real copyright risk, so GrowStream requires strong controls from launch:

- Creator agreements
- Copyright declaration at upload
- Content review before publishing
- Takedown process
- Content standards

**Process:** Creator Upload → Rights Declaration → Content Review → Approval → Publishing. Verified partners may receive expedited approval.
