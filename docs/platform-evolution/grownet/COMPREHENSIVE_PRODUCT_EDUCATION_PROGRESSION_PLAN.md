# MyGrowNet — GrowNet

## Comprehensive Product, Education, Skills, Points, Progression and Rewards Plan

**Product:** GrowNet  
**Platform:** MyGrowNet  
**Model:** Membership + structured education + practical skills development + network development + performance rewards + leadership development  
**Progression:** Seven Education Levels  
**Primary Point Systems:** PB Points and MP Points  
**Primary Rewards:** Position Bonus, Monthly Bonus and qualifying Business Funding Reward  
**Education Model:** GrowNet-owned curriculum + demand-driven specialist training + workshops + institutional partnerships  
**Access Model:** Education and skills services are available to GrowNet members and can be activated voluntarily  

---

# 1. Executive Product Definition

GrowNet is MyGrowNet's member, learning, engagement, development and referral platform.

It provides users with a single GrowNet account through which they can access entrepreneurship education, practical skills training, workshops, specialist training opportunities, participating MyGrowNet content services, referrals, points, progression and rewards.

GrowNet is not positioned publicly as a conventional MLM product. The public acquisition experience should lead with useful content, learning, skills development and participating MyGrowNet services. The underlying GrowNet system manages membership, network development, progression, points and rewards.

The education and skills system has two complementary functions:

1. Develop members through a structured seven-level entrepreneurship curriculum.
2. Identify practical skills that members need and organise targeted training using qualified experts, facilitators and institutional partners.

A member receives access to these opportunities through their GrowNet account. Participation in the education and skills system can be activated voluntarily by the member.

The system must clearly distinguish between:

* what a member purchases
* what a member learns
* what skills a member develops
* what activities a member completes
* what their team does
* what points they accumulate
* what Education Level they have achieved
* what rewards they qualify for
* whether they qualify for business funding

These distinctions must exist in the business rules, database, user interface and compensation engine.

# 5. Membership Package vs Education Level

This distinction must remain strict.

A Membership Package answers:

> What membership package does this person have?

An Education Level answers:

> What stage of development has this member demonstrated?

They are related but not equivalent.

For example:

```text
Membership Package: Business
Education Level: Level 4 - Builder
PB Points: 14,250
MP Points: 2,140
Learning Status: Active
Skills Profile: Digital Marketing + Financial Management
```

A Business membership does not automatically create an Education Level.

Reaching Level 5 does not automatically require purchasing a higher membership package.

# 10. GrowNet Skills & Training System

In addition to the structured seven-level entrepreneurship curriculum, GrowNet should operate a demand-driven Skills & Training system.

The purpose is to identify specific skills that members need and organise training around those needs.

GrowNet should not attempt to produce every possible course itself.

Instead, GrowNet can:

1. Identify skill demand.
2. Validate the demand.
3. Identify qualified experts.
4. Design or commission the training.
5. Organise the programme.
6. Deliver it online, physically or through workshops.
7. Assess participants.
8. Record achievements.
9. Where appropriate, involve an institution for certification.

This creates a responsive training system.

* education requirement
* minimum time
* assessment
* practical requirements

# 25. The Two-Gate Progression Model

GrowNet progression must require two independent gates.

### Position Gate
May include:

* PB requirement
* qualifying team activity
* personal activity
* minimum time
* membership status

### Education Gate
May include:

* curriculum completion
* workshops
* assessment
* practical activity
* mentoring
* required specialist training where applicable

Both gates must be satisfied.

Example:

```text
Position Gate:       ✓
Education Gate:      ✗
Level Advancement:   BLOCKED
Reason:              Required education incomplete
```

A member cannot become a Level 5 or Level 7 leader solely because their team has generated sufficient points.

Likewise, completing the curriculum alone does not satisfy the position requirement.

---

# 26. Minimum Time and Permanence

A minimum period should apply to each Education Level so members cannot rush through the entire ladder immediately.

The default proposed minimum is one month per level, subject to final configuration.

There should be no unnecessary upper limit on how long a member may take.

Once an Education Level has been achieved, it remains part of the member's permanent achievement history.

A lapse in membership may affect current privileges, participation or rewards, but should not erase historical educational achievement.

---

# 27. PB Points: Position Bonus Points

PB is the primary long-term position progression currency.

PB is cumulative and measures qualifying activity toward position progression.

Example:

```text
Current PB:          8,500
Next Requirement:   12,000
Progress:            70.8%
```

PB should not arbitrarily reset each month.

All PB movements must be recorded in an auditable transaction ledger.

---

# 28. MP Points: Monthly Performance Points

MP represents monthly performance.

MP is used primarily to determine Monthly Bonus qualification.

MP:

* operates within a defined compensation cycle
* resets at the beginning of a new cycle
* retains historical records
* can include personal and team performance
* remains separate from PB

---

# 29. PB and MP Separation

One activity can generate both PB and MP.

Example:

```text
Education Subscription
       ↓
+100 PB
+50 MP
```

Another activity may generate only one type.

Example:

```text
Registration
       ↓
PB only
```

The exact values remain configurable.

PB and MP are not cash.

They must remain separate from the financial wallet and accounting ledger.

# 32. Position Bonus

The Position Bonus is a reward associated with achieving a qualifying position.

It should only trigger after the complete qualification process has been verified.

A Position Bonus record should contain:

* member_id
* level achieved
* qualification date
* PB requirement
* education requirements
* qualification result
* bonus amount
* payment status

Purchasing a package must not automatically trigger a Position Bonus.

---

# 33. Monthly Bonus

The Monthly Bonus is calculated independently from the Position Bonus.

It is based primarily on MP and current-cycle qualification.

A member can:

* progress toward a new Education Level
* achieve a new position
* receive a Position Bonus
* separately qualify for a Monthly Bonus

These mechanisms must never be mixed.

# 47. Governing Rules

1. Creating a GrowNet account does not require purchasing a package.
2. Education and Skills access can be activated voluntarily.
3. Buying a membership package does not automatically mean education is completed.
4. Paying for education does not automatically mean the member completed it.
5. Completing training does not automatically create PB or MP unless the applicable rule says so.
6. Team points cannot automatically satisfy personal education requirements.
7. Personal education cannot automatically satisfy the position requirement.
8. A member does not advance solely because they recruited people.
9. A member does not need to read or write to participate in education.
10. Institutional training does not bypass GrowNet's progression requirements.
11. An institutional certificate must accurately identify the issuing institution.
12. GrowNet must not claim accreditation it does not possess.
13. Education achievement remains in the member's historical record.
14. PB and MP are not cash.
15. PB and MP must remain separate from the financial ledger.
16. Position Bonus is not triggered merely by package purchase.
17. Level 5 or Level 6 does not automatically guarantee business funding.
18. Business funding remains subject to its own eligibility and assessment rules.
19. Compensation rules must be configurable and auditable.
20. Every point movement must be traceable.
21. Every education achievement must be traceable.
22. Every certificate must be verifiable.
23. Training demand should be measured before significant resources are committed to new specialist programmes.
24. Expert qualifications should be verified before an expert is approved for official training.
25. The education system should prioritize practical competence rather than content consumption alone.

# 50. Education Level Advancement Service

A dedicated:

`EducationLevelAdvancementService`

should evaluate the education requirements.

A separate progression service should evaluate the overall position qualification.

The final level advancement decision should combine the applicable gates without merging their underlying data.

Conceptually:

```text
Position Qualification
        +
Education Qualification
        +
Minimum Time
        +
Membership Eligibility
        ↓
Level Qualification Result
        ↓

```

The system should retain a clear audit trail explaining why the member qualified or failed to qualify.

# 54. Specialist Training Launch Model

Start with a controlled pilot.

For example:

```text
1. Identify 5 high-demand skills.
2. Validate member interest.
3. Select 2 or 3 experts.
4. Run small training cohorts.
5. Record attendance and completion.
6. Assess learner satisfaction and competence.
7. Test certificate arrangements.
8. Measure cost and revenue.
9. Improve the training model.
10. Expand successful programmes.
```

This prevents GrowNet from spending heavily on courses before demand is established.

# 56. Central Business Rule

> **Points measure qualifying activity. Education Levels represent demonstrated development. Skills training builds practical capability. Workshops provide accessible and applied learning. Institutional partnerships provide additional expertise and, where formally agreed, external certification. Rewards recognize qualifying performance and achievement. Business funding is a separate opportunity for qualifying Level 5 and Level 6 leaders.**

This rule must guide the product design, database structure, user interface, compensation engine and education system.

The objective is to ensure that GrowNet does not become a platform where members simply accumulate points or consume videos.

The intended outcome is a system where members can:

**learn → develop skills → apply knowledge → build businesses → develop leadership → demonstrate performance → progress → qualify for rewards and, where eligible, business funding.**
