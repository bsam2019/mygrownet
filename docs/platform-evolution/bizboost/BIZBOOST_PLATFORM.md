# BizBoost — Platform Architecture & Product Specification

**Document Status**: Architectural Master Plan for BizBoost Module Rewrite  
**Target Module**: BizBoost (`App\Domain\BizBoost\`, `database/migrations/bizboost/`)  
**Integration Scope**: Platform Core, GrowBuilder, BizDocs, StockFlow, GrowMarket, GrowFinance  

---

## Executive Summary & Product Vision

**BizBoost** is a customer engagement and marketing intelligence platform designed for businesses to attract prospects, capture enquiries, manage leads, drive systematic follow-ups, execute targeted communications, and measure actual revenue outcomes generated by their marketing.

BizBoost is architected for both **online and offline businesses**. A business does not require an e-commerce shop to achieve full value from the platform.

### Supported Customer Journeys
- **Advertisement → Website → Enquiry → Follow-up → Quotation → Physical Sale**
- **Advertisement → WhatsApp → Appointment → Visit → Payment**
- **Facebook → Admissions Enquiry → School Visit → Enrolment**
- **Website → Property Enquiry → Viewing → Negotiation → Sale**
- **SMS → Customer Reactivation → Shop Visit → Purchase**

The primary engineering objective is **connecting marketing touchpoints to real business financial outcomes**.

---

## 1. The Problem Statement

SMEs currently operate across disconnected tool silos:
- Facebook (Advertising)
- WhatsApp (Customer Messaging)
- Website (Static Info)
- Excel / Sheets (Customer Records)
- Email (Incoming Enquiries)
- SMS (Promotions)
- Invoicing / Billing Software
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Bank / Mobile Money Accounts

These disconnected tools prevent businesses from answering critical commercial questions:
1. Which marketing campaign produced this paying customer?
2. Which leads have not received a follow-up?
3. Which quotations are outstanding/pending?
4. Which customers are slipping into inactivity?
5. Which customers have the highest repeat-purchase probability?
6. What is our team's average response time to new enquiries?
7. Exactly how much revenue was generated from our marketing spend?
8. Which marketing channel delivers the highest Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)?

BizBoost unifies these silos into a **single customer & marketing intelligence workflow**.

## 3. Core Product Model & Five Pillars

BizBoost structures all capabilities around five core activities:

```text
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│   CAPTURE   │ ──► │ UNDERSTAND  │ ──► │     ACT     │ ──► │   ENGAGE    │ ──► │   MEASURE   │
└─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
  Identify            Identify            Systematic          Multi-channel       Connect to 
  Prospect            Behavior &          Follow-up           Targeted            Revenue & 
  Interest            Context             Pipeline            Campaigns           Marketing ROI
```

1. **Capture**: Identify prospect interest across website, smart forms, AI chat, WhatsApp, phone, and ads.
2. **Understand**: Build unified customer profiles capturing identity, intent, activity history, and lead source.
3. **Act**: Enforce systematic lead management, response-time targets, and automated follow-up reminders.
4. **Engage**: Execute targeted multi-channel communication (SMS, WhatsApp, Email, Social).
5. **Measure**: Attribute digital and physical revenue outcomes directly back to campaigns and channels.

## 8. Website Visitor Intelligence & Tracking Engine

### Event Collection Architecture
BizBoost captures anonymous website activity via lightweight JavaScript SDK (`bizboost-tracker.js`) and converts it into identified customer records upon first interaction.

```text
Visitor ──► Anonymous Session ──► Event Stream ──► Form/WhatsApp Click ──► Identity Resolution ──► Customer Profile
```

### Captured Event Types
- `session_started`, `page_viewed`, `product_viewed`, `pricing_viewed`
- `whatsapp_clicked`, `phone_clicked`, `email_clicked`
- `form_submitted`, `quote_requested`, `appointment_booked`
- `checkout_started`, `purchase_completed`

## 10. Omnichannel Communication & Link Attribution

### Trackable WhatsApp Links
Generates campaign-tagged WhatsApp links (`https://bizboost.link/wa/{hash}`) that record visitor IP, session, campaign parameters, and target product prior to redirecting to WhatsApp.

### Phone Enquiry Tracking
Tracks "Call Now" button clicks and prompts salespeople with a lead-creation dialog to record call details and outcome.

### SMS Infrastructure (Africala Integration)
- **Carrier Unit Rate**: K0.14 per SMS (K14,000 per 100,000 SMS float).
- **Capabilities**: Promotional blasts, automated follow-up reminders, quote expiration alerts, appointment notifications, customer reactivation.

## 11. Sales Productivity & AI Assistant

### Response-Time Monitoring
Tracks duration from `lead_created` to `first_response_logged`. Surfaces SLA compliance metrics:
- Average Response Time
- Unanswered Lead Rate %
- SLA Alert Banners (< 30 min target)

### AI Sales Assistant
Leverages customer session and interaction history to generate:

- **Recommended Follow-up Questions**: Contextual prompts tailored to product intent.
- **Message Drafts**: Personalised SMS/WhatsApp follow-up templates.

## 13. Customer Lifecycle & Retention Management

1. **Customer Segmentation Engine**: Filter by demographics, lead source, interaction history, purchase recency, and CLV.
2. **Customer Reactivation Workflows**: Automatically flag inactive customers (e.g., no purchase in 90 days) and trigger targeted SMS/WhatsApp reactivation campaigns.
3. **Repeat-Purchase Predictor**: Trigger reminders based on consumption cycles (e.g., vehicle service due, salon visit cycle, school term renewal).
4. **Lost Customer Detection**: Alert sales managers when regular purchasing frequency drops significantly.

## 18. Domain-Driven Design (DDD) Technical Architecture

The rewritten module adopts DDD standards under `app/Domain/BizBoost/`:

```text
app/Domain/BizBoost/
├── ValueObjects/           CustomerId, LeadId, CampaignId, LeadScore, Money
├── Entities/               Customer, Lead, LeadActivity, Campaign, TrackableLink, EventLog
├── Repositories/           CustomerRepositoryInterface, LeadRepositoryInterface, CampaignRepositoryInterface
├── Services/               LeadCaptureService, VisitorTrackingService, AttributionEngine, FollowUpScheduler
└── Exceptions/             BizBoostException, LeadAssignmentException, QuotaExceededException

app/Infrastructure/
├── Persistence/Eloquent/BizBoost/     Thin Eloquent data mappers
└── Persistence/Repositories/BizBoost/ Repository implementations

app/Http/Controllers/BizBoost/         Thin controllers delegating to domain services
routes/bizboost.php                    BizBoost route definitions
database/migrations/bizboost/          Module schema migrations
```

---

## 19. Core Database Schema Specifications

The rewrite updates existing tables and introduces canonical DDD entities:

1. `bizboost_customers`: Unified customer profile records.
2. `bizboost_leads`: Pipeline leads, status, lead score, assigned user, source.
3. `bizboost_lead_activities`: Ingestion events, notes, follow-up logs.
4. `bizboost_tracking_events`: Captured website sessions, page views, clicks, form submits.
5. `bizboost_trackable_links`: Campaign-tagged short links for WhatsApp/SMS.
6. `bizboost_campaigns`: SMS/Email/WhatsApp campaigns, schedule, cost, conversion count.
7. `bizboost_attributions`: Conversion events linked to campaign/source touchpoints.
